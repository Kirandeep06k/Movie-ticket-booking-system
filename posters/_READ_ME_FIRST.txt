PUT YOUR MOVIE POSTERS IN THIS FOLDER

1. Save each poster as a JPG or PNG (not WEBP).
2. Rename the file EXACTLY like the list below (capital letters do not matter).
3. Restart the app. Posters appear on the movie cards and the movie page.

Tip: portrait posters (taller than wide) look best. Wide images are cropped from the centre.
If a poster is missing, the card shows a dark placeholder with the movie name.
